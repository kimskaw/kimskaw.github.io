01.

About

I'm a hands-on builder who treats a home network like production. My lab runs twelve services around the clock on a single Raspberry Pi — network-wide ad blocking, intrusion detection, full observability, and workflow automation — with zero ports exposed to the internet.

What I care about is the unglamorous half of infrastructure: auditing what's actually running, closing exposure that "looked fine," and making deliberate tradeoffs between automation and stability. When something breaks, I dig until I understand why — then I write it up.

Everything I ship gets documented, including the mistakes. The write-ups below aren't feature lists; they're what went wrong and what it took to notice.

  • Self-hosted firstIf I can run it myself, I do — and harden it.
  • Security by default"It's just the LAN" is not a boundary.
  • Measure everything"Up" isn't the same as "correct."
  • Document the messWrite-ups include the failures, not just wins.
